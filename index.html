Accounting workflow

How a receivable moves from invoice to report.

  1. InvoiceVerify and record sales invoices against orders and delivery.
  2. PaymentRecord receipts by cash, cheque or bank transfer.
  3. AllocationAllocate each payment to the correct customer invoice.
  4. ReconciliationMatch payments, invoices and bank entries; investigate differences.
  5. AgingTrack overdue, partially paid and outstanding balances.
  6. CollectionFollow up with customers and the sales team.
  7. ReportingPrepare SOA, due and collection reports for month-end.
